Privacy Policy

Homebridge Nest Bridge. Last updated September 22, 2026.

This app is for personal use by its owner only. It uses the Google Smart Device Management API to read and control the owner's own Nest thermostat and show it in Apple HomeKit.

What data is accessed

Thermostat status and settings (temperature, mode, setpoints, online status) for devices the owner has authorized.

How it is used and stored

Data is used only to display and control the thermostat in HomeKit. It is processed on a Raspberry Pi in the owner's home. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page (myaccount.google.com/permissions) or the Nest partner connections page.
